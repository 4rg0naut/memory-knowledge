# Harbor silicon roadmap note (2026-10-14)

The R300 robot will use the Nimbus-12 SoC, which pairs two dies for 128 GB unified memory.

The Nimbus-12 design win was announced at the Harbor DevDay.

Harbor's firmware team in Gdansk maintains the depot sync tooling on the tailnet, replacing the older rsync shell scripts.
