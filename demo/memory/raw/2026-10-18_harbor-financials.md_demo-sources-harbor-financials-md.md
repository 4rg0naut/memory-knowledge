# Harbor Robotics financial note (2026-10-05)

Harbor Robotics reported FY2025 revenue of 4.2M EUR, up 38% year over year.

The company closed a Series B of 30M EUR led by Delft Ventures.

Headquarters remain in Rotterdam; the company opened a second office in Gdansk for firmware work.
